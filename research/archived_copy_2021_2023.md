## ATTRIBUTION WARNING for anyone reusing this file
The scraped Play pages also carried "similar apps" blocks and user reviews. The
following were found on those pages but belong to OTHER apps and must NOT be
attributed to Calm or Headspace:
- "now free for your first year" / "$399 lifetime" -> Balance
- "The number 1 free meditation app" / "100+ new free guided meditations added daily" -> Insight Timer
- "BetterSleep helps you understand and improve your sleep" -> BetterSleep
Lines quoted above as VERBATIM were checked to sit inside the target app's own
description block.
